Business Overview & Financial Performance
MCX is India's national monopoly commodity derivatives exchange — 95.9%+ market share in non-agri commodities. Revenue = ADTV × fee rate (basis points). The business model has extreme operating leverage: 107% revenue growth → 138% PAT growth in FY26. The FY22-24 trough was entirely explained by the platform transition (Project Udaan: TCS/Deutsche Boerse T7 platform). FY25-26 represents post-transition normalization + volume acceleration.
