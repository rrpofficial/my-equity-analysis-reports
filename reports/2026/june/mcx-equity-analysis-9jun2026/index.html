    MCX — Multi Commodity Exchange of India Limited¶

    Comprehensive Equity Research Report¶

    NSE: MCX | BSE: 534091 | CMP: ₹2,824.80 | Date: 9 June 2026
    Sources: Screener.in, Trendlyne, NSE India, BSE India, MCX IR, Business Standard, Multibagg.ai — all fetched live, 9 June 2026. No LLM training data used for any financial figure.

    ⚠ Critical Context — MCX's Unique Structure¶

    Three structural facts that make MCX unlike any other Indian stock:

    1. Zero promoter holding — MCX has no promoter. Original promoter FTIL (63 Moons) was ordered by SEBI to divest after the 2013 NSEL payment default. FTIL has fully exited. MCX is 100% institutionally/publicly held — zero promoter diversion risk.
    2. SEBI-regulated Market Infrastructure Institution (MII) — Board composition, risk management, technology, and governance are under continuous SEBI oversight. Strongest form of oversight for any listed entity.
    3. NSEL legacy is resolved — MCX was a separate entity from NSEL. MCX has been cleared of direct culpability. The historical overhang contributed to the 2014-2022 trough but is no longer a live issue.

    1.3 Governance Score: STRONG¶

    MCX has the strongest possible governance profile for an Indian listed company: no promoter (zero fund-diversion risk), direct SEBI oversight as an MII, professional board, planned auditor rotation. The only structural consideration is the "no strategic anchor" risk — absence of a committed long-term promoter could create strategic drift or M&A vulnerability (mitigated by SEBI's approval power over exchanges).

    Step 4 — Forensic Accounting & Governance Quality¶

    Hard Reject Check: PASS — No triggers¶

    Preliminary MRS (tool): 🔴 RED (two automated flags)¶

    Context-Adjusted MRS: 🟡 AMBER (both flags explained by verified one-time events)¶

    Flag 1 — DSO-Revenue Divergence FY24 (Tool: Red) - DSO jumped from 10 → 33 days in FY24; tool flagged 230pp divergence - Explanation: MCX's platform migration (Oct 2023) temporarily disrupted settlement/receivables cycles. SEBI halted the migration due to technical issues; the transition window created short-term receivable pile-up. - Evidence: DSO recovered to 9 days (FY25) and 17 days (FY26) — no sustained pattern. Systematic manipulation would show persistent elevation. - Verdict: ✅ One-time operational disruption. Not a revenue manipulation signal.

    Flag 2 — CWIP Aging FY22-23 (Tool: Red) - CWIP was 37.8% of fixed assets (FY22) and 53% (FY23) - Explanation: Exactly Project Udaan — MCX contracted TCS in 2022 to build the T7 trading platform. CWIP of ₹95 Cr (FY22) and ₹169 Cr (FY23) is the platform under construction. - Evidence: CWIP fell to ₹16 Cr in FY24 upon capitalization — asset was completed and deployed. - Verdict: ✅ Confirmed infrastructure investment with documented completion. Not stalled CWIP.

    5.3 Reverse DCF — What Growth Is Priced In?¶

    At ₹2,825 with 13% discount rate and 5% terminal growth: market implies ~25-30% sustained PAT growth for 5-7 years. MCX's 5Y historical PAT CAGR is 49% — so the embedded expectations are actually below demonstrated performance. This argues the valuation is not stretched relative to history.
