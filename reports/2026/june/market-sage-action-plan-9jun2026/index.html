    Analytical Notes & Policy Rules Applied¶

    Large-Cap Avoidance Rule¶

    No new individual large-cap stocks, as the user's ₹5–5.5L in Nifty 50, Nifty Next 50, and large-cap MFs already provides top-100 market coverage.     LIC Shareholding Policy¶

    Per explicit analytical policy: LIC (Life Insurance Corporation of India) shareholding is not cited or used as a governance signal. LIC investments are subject to political direction rather than independent investment merit (evidence: Adani 2023 crisis purchases, ITC tobacco, Rajesh Exports). Only private domestic institutional holdings (SBI MF, HDFC MF, Kotak MF, ICICI Pru MF, Axis MF, Nippon MF etc.) are treated as genuine institutional validation.

    DII Decomposition Rule¶

    When DII % appears high in any shareholding pattern analysis, it is decomposed into "LIC" vs "Other private DIIs." Only private MFs and insurance companies (not LIC) are counted as positive governance signals.
