## ⚠ Critical Context — MCX's Unique Structure

Three structural facts that make MCX unlike any other Indian stock:

1. **Zero promoter holding** — MCX has no promoter. Original promoter FTIL (63 Moons) was ordered by SEBI to divest after the 2013 NSEL payment default. FTIL has fully exited. MCX is 100% institutionally/publicly held — zero promoter diversion risk.
2. **SEBI-regulated Market Infrastructure Institution (MII)** — Board composition, risk management, technology, and governance are under continuous SEBI oversight. Strongest form of oversight for any listed entity.
3. **NSEL legacy is resolved** — MCX was a separate entity from NSEL. MCX has been cleared of direct culpability. The historical overhang contributed to the 2014-2022 trough but is no longer a live issue.

## Step 4 — Forensic Accounting & Governance Quality

### Hard Reject Check: **PASS — No triggers**

### Preliminary MRS (tool): 🔴 RED (two automated flags)
### Context-Adjusted MRS: 🟡 AMBER (both flags explained by verified one-time events)

**Flag 1 — DSO-Revenue Divergence FY24 (Tool: Red)**
- DSO jumped from 10 → 33 days in FY24; tool flagged 230pp divergence
- **Explanation**: MCX's platform migration (Oct 2023) temporarily disrupted settlement/receivables cycles. SEBI halted the migration due to technical issues; the transition window created short-term receivable pile-up.
- **Evidence**: DSO recovered to **9 days (FY25)** and **17 days (FY26)** — no sustained pattern. Systematic manipulation would show persistent elevation.
- **Verdict**: ✅ One-time operational disruption. Not a revenue manipulation signal.

**Flag 2 — CWIP Aging FY22-23 (Tool: Red)**
- CWIP was 37.8% of fixed assets (FY22) and 53% (FY23)
- **Explanation**: Exactly Project Udaan — MCX contracted TCS in 2022 to build the T7 trading platform. CWIP of ₹95 Cr (FY22) and ₹169 Cr (FY23) is the platform under construction.
- **Evidence**: CWIP fell to ₹16 Cr in FY24 upon capitalization — asset was completed and deployed.
- **Verdict**: ✅ Confirmed infrastructure investment with documented completion. Not stalled CWIP.
